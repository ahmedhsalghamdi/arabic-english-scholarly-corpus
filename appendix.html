Why this is a calque
يلعب دوراً calques the theatrical English metaphor "play a role". Pre-modern Arabic expressed functional agency through يقوم مقام (stands in the place of), كان شأنه عظيماً (its matter was great), or له أثر (it has an effect). The collocational frame دور + adjective mirrors English "[adj.] role" systematically.
